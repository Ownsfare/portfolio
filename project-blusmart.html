BluSmart Insolvency System

A multi-tenant platform streamlining the insolvency claims process with row-level locking for financial integrity.

99.9% Data Accuracy
Hrs Reduced from Days
100% TypeScript Coverage

The Challenge

Traditional insolvency processes suffer from manual paper-based delays, lack of transparency for creditors, and significant compliance risks in tracking voting events.

The Solution

I architected a secure, multi-tenant SaaS platform that digitizes claim submission, automates verification, and enables tamper-proof e-voting for the Committee of Creditors.

Key Innovations

  • 🔹 Row-Level Locking: Ensured ACID compliance for bulk disbursements to prevent double-spending.
  • 🔹 Case-Scoped Isolation: Custom middleware to strictly separate data between different insolvency cases.
  • 🔹 Automated E-Voting: Real-time tallying of votes based on complex "voting share" logic derived from admitted claim amounts.
